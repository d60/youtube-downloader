Welcome to Ytdler.com!

Ytdler.com is a free tool for downloading YouTube videos in a variety of formats, including MP4, Webm, MP3, and more. With a simple and fast process, you can easily download your favorite videos in just a few clicks.

How to Use Ytdler.com

Step 1: Enter the Video URL

To begin, paste the URL of the YouTube video you want to download into the input field. After entering the link, click the Download button to fetch the video information.

Step 2: Select Your Preferred Format

Once the video details are retrieved, you’ll see a list of available formats. Choose the one that fits your needs—whether it's MP4, MP3, or WebM.

Step 3: Specify the File Name

Before downloading, you can specify a custom file name for the video. Use the following placeholders to dynamically include video details:

  • [title]: The title of the video
  • [id]: The video ID
  • [channel]: The name of the uploader

For example, a file name like "[title] - [channel]" will generate a file name such as "My Video - My Channel.mp4".

Step 4: Download the Video

After selecting your preferred format and specifying the file name (if desired), click the Download button. The download will begin automatically, and the video will be saved to your device.
